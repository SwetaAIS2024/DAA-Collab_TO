REFERENCE: CANONICAL INTENTS (10 total)
================================================================================

4.  incident_classification - Categorizing incident types
5.  traffic_impact          - Effect on traffic flow, delays, congestion
6.  traffic_anomaly         - Unusual patterns, outliers, spikes
7.  causal_analysis         - Why/reasons/causes of incidents
8.  traffic_forecasting     - Predictions and estimates
9.  visualization           - Charts, graphs, maps, plots
10. report_generation       - Generating reports and summaries
